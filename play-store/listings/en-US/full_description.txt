Chef is your personal cooking assistant. Tell it what you're in the mood for, what's in your fridge, or how much time you have, and Chef suggests recipes that suit you. Save the ones you like and build your own collection.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH CHEF

• Chat your way to dinner. Ask for ideas like "a quick vegetarian weeknight dinner" or "something with the leftover chicken". Chef answers with recipe cards you can open, compare and save.
• Talk hands-free. Use your voice while you cook, and Chef reads its answers back to you.
• Complete recipes. Each recipe has ingredients, step-by-step instructions, prep and cooking time, difficulty, servings, nutrition per serving, and tips & tricks, plus an AI-generated photo.
• Make it yours. Ask Chef to adjust any recipe (make it vegan, double it, swap an ingredient) and save the result as your own variant.
• Organise your collection. Save favourites, search and filter by tags such as cuisine, main ingredient or cooking time, and group recipes into lists like "Work week" or "Dinner party".
• Discover something new. Browse the shared recipe collection, see the Recipe of the Month, and get AI-curated cooking resources on the home screen.
• Personal suggestions. Chef picks up your preferences over time, like dietary needs or favourite cuisines, so its suggestions fit you better.
• Chef everywhere. The chat button is on every screen, so help is always one tap away.

GOOD TO KNOW

Chef is in early beta. To keep costs under control, each verified account gets a limited number of AI interactions for now. You can browse recipes as a guest without an account.

Recipes, tips and chat answers are generated by AI and can contain mistakes, including wrong cooking times or temperatures. Always use your own judgment on food safety and allergens.

We don't sell your data. You can delete your account at any time from within the app.
