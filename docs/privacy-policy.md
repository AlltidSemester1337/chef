# Chef — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-08

Chef ("the app", "we", "us") is a cooking-assistant Android app. It is operated and maintained by Karl Enberg, a private individual in Sweden, who is the data controller. This policy explains what data Chef collects, why, who processes it, and how you can have it deleted.

Contact: **humlekottekonsult@gmail.com**

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Email address and password** (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it) | When you create an account | To sign you in and verify your email |
| **Chat messages** you type or speak, and Chef's replies | When you use the chat | To generate recipes and answers, and to keep your chat history between sessions |
| **Recipes, variants and lists** you save | When you save or organise recipes | To show your collection. Saved recipes are part of a shared recipe collection that other Chef users can browse |
| **Detected cooking preferences** (e.g. "vegetarian, likes spicy food") | Derived automatically from your chats | To personalise suggestions |
| **Liked messages** | When you like a reply | To remember replies you liked |
| **Usage counter** of AI interactions | Each AI request | To enforce the beta usage limit |
| **App usage and device data** (app interactions, app-instance ID, device model, OS version) | Automatically, via Firebase Analytics | To understand how the app is used and improve it |
| **Diagnostic traces** of AI requests (the prompt, the model's reply, timing) | Each AI request | To monitor and evaluate the quality of AI answers |

**Voice input:** when you use the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your device's built-in Android speech recognition service (for example Google's). Chef receives only the resulting text and never stores audio recordings. The speech service your device provides may process audio under its own privacy policy.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment information. Chef has no ads.

## 2. Third parties that process your data

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf, only for the purposes described here:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage, Analytics, App Check): account, stored content, and usage analytics. Recipes, chat history and preferences are stored in Cloud Firestore in Stockholm, Sweden (`europe-north2`). The previous Firebase Realtime Database copy is kept read-only for at most 30 days after the move (October 2026) and then deleted. [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Cloud Vertex AI / Imagen**: generating recipe images from recipe descriptions.
- **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech**: reading Chef's replies aloud. The reply text is sent for synthesis.
- **Berget.ai**: the large language model that powers chat, recipe extraction and preference detection. Your chat messages are sent to Berget.ai to generate replies. [Berget.ai privacy](https://berget.ai)
- **Arize Phoenix**: storing diagnostic traces of AI requests (prompts and replies) for quality evaluation.

We do **not** sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

## 3. Storage, security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Account data, chat history, preferences and lists are kept while your account exists. Diagnostic traces are kept for up to 300 days. Analytics data is kept according to Firebase Analytics' default retention settings.

## 4. Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app, or by emailing us. See **[How to delete your Chef account and data](delete-account.md)** for what is deleted and what is kept.

## 5. AI-generated content

Recipes, tips and chat replies are generated by AI and can contain mistakes, including wrong cooking times, temperatures, or allergen information. Always use your own judgment about food safety.

## 6. Children

Chef is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Your rights

If you live in the EU/EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden, IMY).

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
