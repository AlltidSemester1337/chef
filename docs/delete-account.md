# How to delete your Chef account and data

Chef is developed and operated by Karl Enberg. You can delete your Chef account and its associated data in either of these ways.

## Option 1: In the app (immediate)

1. Open Chef and sign in.
2. On the **Home** screen, scroll to the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. If Chef asks you to sign in again first (a security requirement for recently inactive sessions), sign out, sign back in, and repeat the steps.

## Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Send an email to **humlekottekonsult@gmail.com** from the address you use with Chef, with the subject **"Delete my Chef account"**. We will delete your account and data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email address and credentials)
- Your chat history, liked messages, recipe lists, detected preferences, and usage counter.
  - **Email option:** deleted together with your account.
  - **In-app option:** these can no longer be accessed once the account is gone, but the app does not yet remove them automatically. To have them erased, also send the email described in Option 2 and we will delete them within 30 days.

## What is kept

- **Recipes you saved to the shared recipe collection** stay available to other Chef users. After deletion they are no longer linked to an account you can access. If you want them removed too, say so in your deletion email.
- Analytics data and diagnostic traces are kept for a limited time (traces up to 300 days) and then deleted automatically.

See also the [Chef Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
